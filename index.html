Hi, I'm Midnight.

I spend my days teaching computers new tricks, my evenings arguing with a terminal that lives inside a Linux box I'm far too attached to, and my nights convinced that one more idea before bed is always a good idea. I take photos with cameras older than some of my code, model little worlds in Blender, train models that occasionally do what I ask, and build things mostly because I can't help it. Somewhere between a keyboard, a camera, and a sketchy 3am hypothesis, that's me — probably a little too curious for my own good, and completely at peace with that.
